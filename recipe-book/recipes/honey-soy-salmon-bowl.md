## Ingredients

### Salmon

- 2 portions salmon, with or without skin
- ⅓ cup soy sauce
- ⅓ cup honey
- 4-5 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tsp minced ginger
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tbsp red pepper flakes
- Sesame seeds, to finish

### Bowl

- Cooked rice
- Cucumber
- Avocado
- Green onion
- Spicy mayo
- Chili crisp

## Steps

1. Cube the salmon and toss it in a bowl with the soy sauce, honey, garlic, ginger, onion powder and red pepper flakes.
2. Heat a skillet over medium-high. Add the salmon and all of the sauce and cook 3 to 4 minutes, then flip and cook another 3 to 4 minutes. Finish with sesame seeds.
3. Build your bowls with rice, the salmon, and toppings: cucumber, avocado, green onion, spicy mayo and chili crisp.

## Notes

Swap in any toppings you like. The sauce reduces into a glaze in the pan, so spoon any extra over the rice.
