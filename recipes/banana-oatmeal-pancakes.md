## Ingredients

### Fridge

- 2 eggs
- ½ cup (120g) unsweetened almond milk

### Pantry

- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 ½ cups (143g) old-fashioned rolled oats (use certified gluten-free if needed)
- 2 tsp baking powder
- ½ tsp ground cinnamon
- ¼ tsp salt
- Olive oil, for the pan

### Counter

- 2 medium ripe bananas (the browner, the better)

## Steps

1. Put everything except the oil in a blender and run it on high for 30 to 60 seconds, until smooth. Leave the batter in the blender while the pan heats up.
2. Lightly oil a griddle or skillet over medium heat. Pour about ⅓ cup of batter per pancake and cook 2 to 4 minutes, until the edges bubble and the tops puff up a little.
3. Flip and cook until the second side is golden. If they brown too fast, turn the heat down.
4. Wipe the pan, add a little more oil, and repeat with the rest of the batter. Makes about 9 pancakes.
