## Ingredients

### Fridge

- 3 tbsp butter
- 1/2 cup grated parmesan
- chopped fresh parsley, for topping

### Pantry

- 8 oz spaghetti
- salt, for the pasta water
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp salt

### Counter

- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 lemon

## Steps

1. Boil the spaghetti in well-salted water until al dente. Scoop out 1/2 cup of the pasta water before draining.
2. Melt the butter in a pan over medium-low heat, add the minced garlic and cook for about 1 minute.
3. Add the drained noodles, a splash of the pasta water, the parmesan, pepper and 1 tsp salt.
4. Toss until the sauce turns glossy, adding more pasta water if it looks dry.
5. Top with parsley and a squeeze of lemon.
