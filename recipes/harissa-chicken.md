## Ingredients

### Fridge

- Boneless, skinless chicken thighs or breasts
- Harissa paste, plus extra to serve
- Plum tomatoes
- Fresh cilantro, chopped, to garnish

### Pantry

- Honey
- Olive oil
- Paprika
- Black pepper
- Kosher salt

### Counter

- 4 lemons (1 for the marinade, 3 for grilling)

## Steps

1. Whisk the harissa, honey, olive oil, paprika, black pepper and the juice of one lemon together in a bowl.
2. Pat the chicken dry, cut a few shallow slits into the thickest parts, and salt both sides. Toss it in the marinade until coated, then cover and chill for at least 30 minutes.
3. Halve the remaining three lemons, cut the tomatoes, and heat the grill to about 350-375°F.
4. Grill the chicken about 4 minutes per side. For the last 3 minutes, put the lemon halves and tomatoes on the grill too.
5. Let everything rest for about 5 minutes, then scatter with cilantro and serve with extra harissa.

## Notes

Marinate 30 to 60 minutes; more than about 4 hours is too long, and after a day the lemon makes the chicken mushy. For a milder version, stir Greek yogurt into the harissa (this makes it no longer dairy-free). Brands of harissa vary in heat and smokiness. The source gives no quantities for most ingredients, so adjust to taste; a reader tip suggests the original was written for about 2 lb of chicken.
