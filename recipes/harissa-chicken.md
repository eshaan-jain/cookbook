## Ingredients

### Fridge

- Boneless, skinless chicken thighs or breasts
- Harissa paste, plus extra to serve
- Plum tomatoes
- Fresh cilantro, chopped, to garnish

### Pantry

- Honey
- Olive oil
- Paprika
- Black pepper
- Kosher salt

### Counter

- 4 lemons (1 for the marinade, 3 for grilling)

## Steps

1. Whisk the harissa, honey, olive oil, paprika, black pepper and the juice of one lemon together in a bowl.
2. Pat the chicken dry, cut a few shallow slits into the thickest parts, and salt both sides. Turn it in the marinade until coated, then cover and chill for at least 30 minutes.
3. Halve the remaining three lemons and heat the grill to about 350-375°F.
4. Grill the chicken about 4 minutes per side. For the last 3 minutes, add the lemon halves and tomatoes to the grill.
5. Let everything rest for about 5 minutes, then scatter with cilantro and serve with extra harissa.
