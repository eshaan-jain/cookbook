## Ingredients

### Fridge

- 1 lb extra-lean (99%) ground turkey or chicken
- 1 medium red bell pepper, chopped
- Shredded cheese (optional)
- Sour cream (optional)
- Cilantro (optional)

### Pantry

- 2 tsp olive oil
- 4 tbsp chili powder (mild brand; if yours is hotter, start with 2 tbsp)
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- ¼ tsp cayenne pepper
- ½ tsp salt, plus more to taste
- 1 (28 oz) can diced or crushed tomatoes
- 1 ¼ cups chicken broth
- 2 (15 oz) cans dark red kidney beans, rinsed and drained
- 1 (15 oz) can sweet corn, rinsed and drained
- Tortilla chips (optional)

### Counter

- 1 yellow onion, chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- Avocado (optional)

## Steps

1. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium-high heat. Cook the onion, garlic and bell pepper for 5 to 7 minutes, until softened.
2. Add the turkey and break it up as it cooks, until no longer pink. Stir in the chili powder, cumin, oregano, cayenne and salt and cook for about 20 seconds.
3. Add the tomatoes, broth, beans and corn. Bring to a boil, then turn the heat down and simmer 30 to 45 minutes, until thick. Taste and adjust the seasoning.
4. Ladle into bowls and add whatever toppings you like.

## Notes

- **Slow cooker:** brown the turkey and onion first, use only ½ cup of broth, then cook on high 3 to 4 hours or low 6 to 7 hours.
- Chili powder brands vary a lot in heat, so add it gradually.
- Leftovers keep 3 to 4 days in the fridge, or up to 3 months in the freezer.
